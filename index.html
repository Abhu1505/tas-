ℹ️ How to use your portal — quick guide
  1. 🏋️ My Plan shows every exercise your trainer assigned, with target sets × reps, weight and rest. The green bar tracks how many plan exercises you've completed in finished sessions.
  2. 📅 Your upcoming schedule lists your next classes with the time set by your trainer. Tap ✏️ Edit on any day to update your exercises — your edits are saved as proposals and only appear in the official log after your trainer approves them. Tap 🚫 Cancel to cancel a class (the trainer's approval is needed before it disappears from the schedule). Times stay set by your trainer.
  3. 🕓 Approval flow: cancelled-class requests show an "awaiting approval" chip until your trainer decides; they appear under ✅ Approvals → 📅 Schedule where the trainer approves or declines with a note.
  4. ✅ Everything is trainer-approved. Your exercise edits and class cancellations never apply directly — the trainer confirms them first, so the official log always stays accurate.
  5. 📜 History lists all your past workouts — both days with logged exercises and every finished session. Tap a day to expand it; use ✏️ Edit to propose corrections.
  6. 📈 Progress: add weight, measurements and photos here. Client entries go to your trainer for approval before they appear officially.
  7. 👤 Profile: check your details and goals. Edits also need trainer approval.
  8. Use 🔄 Refresh at the top if your trainer just changed something and you don't see it yet.

ℹ️ Trainer quick guide — how everything works
  1. ➕ Create client: fill name + WhatsApp and press Create Client, then share the generated Login ID & password. Passwords are permanent.
  2. 📊 Sessions tab: pick a month, press ⏰ Time to schedule a class for a day, and tick the checkbox to mark a session finished. Finished days lock their workout log.
  3. 🏋️ Exercises (Template): tick library exercises to assign them to this client and optionally override sets/reps/weight/rest/notes — the client sees exactly this under My Plan.
  4. 📝 Log: open any day to add/edit exercises. To edit a locked (finished) day, use the 🔓 Unlock for editing switch inside that day's log.
  5. ✅ Approvals: review client profile edits, progress entries and workout-edit requests here; approving applies them instantly to the client portal.
  6. 📢 Broadcast / 🔔 Notify: send a message to all clients and enable browser alerts for new approval requests.
  7. Press 🔄 Refresh anytime to pull the latest cloud data.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Team Abhi Sutaria — Personal Training, Dubai, UAE

What we collect. Your name, contact details (phone/email), gender, birth date, training goal, medical notes and an emergency contact — plus your session attendance, exercise logs, measurements and progress photos. Nothing else; the portal shows no ads and uses no tracking cookies.

Why we collect it. Solely to plan and deliver your personal-training programme, track your progress, send session reminders/reports you opt into, and meet safety obligations. Medical notes are used only to keep your training safe.

Who sees it. Only you and the head coach (admin). Data is stored in our private cloud database (Supabase) with per-user access control; progress media is served through private Google Drive links that only authenticated users of this portal can open.

Sharing & messaging. Session links and reminders are sent to you over WhatsApp/email at your request. We never sell or rent your data, and we share it with no third parties for marketing.

Retention & deletion. Records are kept while you are an active client. You may ask the admin at any time to export or permanently delete your data — requests are actioned within 7 days.

Security. Password-protected login, HTTPS-only transport and least-privilege access. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.
